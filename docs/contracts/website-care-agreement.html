This agreement is between Trashbox LLC (“Trashbox”) and the Client named above. It covers monthly hosting, care, and the Trashbox form / CRM plan for the website listed above.

1. Monthly fees

Client pays $30.00 per month in advance, billed by Trashbox (typically by card / Stripe). The fee is three separate items:

Item Amount What it covers
Hosting $5 / mo The live site on Trashbox’s hosting (currently Vercel under the Trashbox team), HTTPS, and deploys Trashbox runs.
Care $15 / mo Up to five combined bug fixes and minor content or UI tweaks per month on this site (see Section 3).
Form / CRM plan $10 / mo Trashbox Form API Solo for this site: form submissions, lead inbox, and the site’s API key, within published Solo limits.

Trashbox may show these as one $30 charge or as three line items. Either way, the amounts above apply. Sales tax is added if required by law.

2. What is not included

The $30 monthly fee does not include:

  1. New pages, redesigns, new features, or new form fields.
  2. Copywriting, photography, SEO campaigns, or ads.
  3. The Client’s own email hosting, domain registrar fees, or extra domains.
  4. Moving the site onto the Client’s own Vercel (or other) account, except as a paid handoff under Section 8.
  5. Trashbox Form API Team (extra seats, higher limits, submitter confirmation emails) or extra organizations.
  6. 24/7 support, on-site work, or Trashbox staffing the Client’s inbox.

Work outside this list is a separate quote. Trashbox will not start that work until the Client agrees to the price in writing (email is enough).

3. Care (the $15)

Each billing month includes up to five (5) combined items: bug fixes and/or minor tweaks. Unused items do not roll over. Extra items that month are a separate quote.

A bug is something that used to work and stopped, or a defect in work Trashbox already delivered: the form not sending, a broken layout, the site down, a failed deploy Trashbox controls.

A minor tweak is a small change to the existing site: text or copy swaps, swapping an image the Client provides, links, hours, phone numbers, and other minor UI adjustments (spacing, color, type size, hiding or showing something already on the page). “Minor” means it does not add a page, a new section, a new feature, or a new integration. Trashbox decides in good faith whether a request is minor. If it is not, Trashbox will say so and quote it before doing the work.

A change request is new work: a new section, a new page, a redesign, “make it look more modern,” or new integrations. Change requests are not covered by care.

The Client reports items by email to contact@trashbox.io (or another address Trashbox names). Trashbox typically starts within one to two business days and will start no later than seven business days. That is when work begins, not a promise the item is finished in that window.

4. Hosting (the $5)

The site stays on hosting Trashbox controls unless the parties agree to a transfer. Trashbox is not the domain registrar unless that is written separately. The Client is responsible for keeping domain login and DNS access available when Trashbox needs them.

Hosting does not include a separate mailbox (Google, Microsoft, etc.) for the Client.

5. Form / CRM plan (the $10)

Trashbox will provision a Form API organization and project for the Client’s email and connect the website form. The Client signs up at the Trashbox portal with that email, creates their own password, and is the owner of that organization.

Solo includes, as Trashbox publishes them at the time of this agreement, about 500 form submissions per month and one seat. Going over the limit, adding seats, or moving to Team is extra and may require a Trashbox plan change. Trashbox is not responsible for lost leads if the Client hits the cap or if a third-party outage occurs.

The $10 is paid to Trashbox as part of this agreement. The Client should not also pay Trashbox Form API checkout for the same Solo plan unless the parties switch to a handoff under Section 8.

The Client is responsible for how they use leads, for their own privacy policy and spam practices, and for connecting their mailbox in the portal if they want replies from their own email.

7. Payment, pause, and cancel

Monthly fees are due in advance. If payment fails, Trashbox will notify the Client. If the amount stays unpaid seven days after notice, Trashbox may pause the website and/or the form plan until payment is current.

Either party may cancel monthly care with 30 days’ written notice (email is enough). Trashbox will not refund a month that has already started unless Trashbox agrees in writing. After cancel, hosting and form access continue only if the Client still pays for those items or completes a handoff.

8. Handoff

If the Client leaves or takes the site in-house:

  1. Form. The Client already owns the Trashbox portal login. They may put their own card on the Form API Solo (or other) plan. The $10 line on this agreement then stops.
  2. Hosting. Trashbox will, on request and after unpaid invoices are cleared, help transfer the GitHub repo, Vercel project, and/or DNS instructions. Transfer work may be billed if it is more than a simple handoff. Or the Client may keep paying the $5 hosting line.
  3. Care. The $15 line stops on the cancel date. Further fixes are quoted separately.
  4. API key. If hosting moves off Trashbox, the Client (or Trashbox at handoff) should rotate the form API key and put the new key on the new host. Keys are shown once and must not be committed to a public repo.

9. Client responsibilities

The Client will supply content, timely feedback, and access (domain, DNS, brand files) reasonably needed for Trashbox to do the work. The Client warrants they have the right to use materials they give Trashbox. The Client is responsible for the accuracy of their business information and for legal pages on the site (privacy, terms) unless Trashbox is hired separately to draft them.

10. Third parties and limits

The site and form depend on third parties (including Vercel, Stripe, and the Trashbox Form API). Trashbox is not liable for their outages, changes, or data loss. Trashbox’s total liability under this agreement is limited to the monthly fees the Client actually paid in the one month before the claim. Trashbox is not liable for lost profits, lost leads, or indirect damages.

Trashbox provides the work “as is” after delivery, except for the care described in Section 3 while the Client is current on care fees.
